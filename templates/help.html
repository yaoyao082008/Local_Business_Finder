Help & FAQ

01. How do I add a business?

To add a business, you must first sign or register for an account then an Add Business button will pop up and you can add your business.

02. How do I delete my business

You can only delete a business that you created. There is a red trash can icon in the bottom right of your business image.

03. How do I leave reviews

Each user can only leave 1 review per business. Simply click into the business and click add review

03. Where do I see my businesses and reviews?

Just click on account and click on account settings, in there you can change your password, see your reviews and businesses.
